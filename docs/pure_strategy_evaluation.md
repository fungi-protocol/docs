# 🤖 Pure Strategy Evaluation

How the cost of a pure strategy is evaluated, in two parts. The [Cost function structure](cost_function_structure.md) chapter describes the structure shared by the family of cost functions: time dependence, the linear combination of terms, and bracketing. The [Privacy liability terms](cost_function_concrete.md) chapter summarizes the terms, each of which is specified in its own chapter.
